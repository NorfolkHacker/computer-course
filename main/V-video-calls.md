## The Operator says

I enjoy video calls. Specifically, I enjoy the first four minutes, where nine people say "can you hear me?" to each other and nobody can. I schedule mine for exactly then.

The truth is, a video call is three buttons: microphone, camera and leave. Learn which is which, and you can see the grandchildren, see the doctor, and join the meeting without first showing everyone the inside of your nose. It is also, for a lot of people, how family stays family. So let's do it properly.

## The Mender's way

A **video call** is a phone call where you can see each other. You need a camera (the **webcam**, at the top of the screen, or the phone's front camera), a microphone, and an internet connection.

### The three buttons

Every video call app has the same three controls, usually in a bar along the bottom of the screen. Move the mouse or tap the screen if you can't see them.

| Button | Looks like | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Microphone** (**Mute** / **Unmute**) | A microphone. A line through it means others **can't** hear you. | Turns your sound on and off. |
| **Camera** (**Video** on / off) | A video camera. A line through it means others **can't** see you. | Turns your picture on and off. |
| **Leave** (or **End call**) | Red, often a phone handset | Ends the call. Press it only when you mean it. |

### WhatsApp video calls on your phone

1. Open **WhatsApp**.
2. Tap the chat with the person you want to call.
3. Tap the **video camera** icon at the top right.
4. The first time, your phone asks to use the camera and microphone. Tap **While using the app** for both.
5. To hang up, tap the red **end call** button.

To answer an incoming WhatsApp video call, swipe up or tap the green button (it varies by phone).

### Joining a Teams, Zoom or Google Meet call from a link

Doctors, schools and clubs usually send a **link** by email or text. You don't need an account to join.

### On Windows

1. Click the link in the email. Your web browser opens.
2. If it offers to open an app you haven't got, choose the browser option instead:
   - **Teams**: click **Continue on this browser**.
   - **Zoom**: click **Launch meeting**, then **Join from your browser**.
   - **Google Meet**: it opens in the browser already.
3. A box at the top of the browser asks to use your camera and microphone. Click **Allow**.
4. Type your name if asked.
5. You'll see a preview of yourself. Check the camera and microphone buttons are how you want them.
6. Click **Join now** (or **Ask to join**). You may wait in a "lobby" until the host lets you in.

### On Android

1. Tap the link in the email or text.
2. Your phone may offer to install the app (**Teams**, **Zoom** or **Meet**) from the **Play Store**. This is the easiest way on a phone. Install it, then tap the link again.
3. When the app asks to use the camera and microphone, tap **While using the app**.
4. Type your name if asked, then tap **Join now** (or **Join**).

### If they can't see or hear you

1. Look at the microphone and camera buttons. A line through them means off. Click or tap to turn them on.
2. If you clicked **Block** by mistake in the browser, click the small icon at the left end of the address bar, and set **Camera** and **Microphone** to **Allow**. Then reload the page.
3. On Windows, check the computer itself allows it: **Start** → **Settings** → **Privacy & security** → **Camera** (and then **Microphone**). Make sure **Camera access** and **Let apps access your camera** are **On**.
4. Some laptops have a little sliding cover over the webcam, or a key with a camera symbol that switches it off. Check.

### Looking and sounding good

- **Light in front of you, not behind.** Sit facing a window or lamp, never with it behind you.
- **Camera at eye level.** Prop the laptop on some books. Phone users: lean the phone against something rather than holding it at chin height.
- **Sit back.** Head and shoulders in the picture, about an arm's length away.
- **Headphones stop echo.** If others hear themselves back, wear headphones.
- **Mute when not talking** in a group call, so they don't hear your kettle.

### Leaving the call

Click or tap the red **Leave** (Teams), **Leave** then **Leave Meeting** (Zoom), or the red phone **Leave call** (Meet). Close the browser tab afterwards.

## Do it now

1. On Windows, click **Start**, type *Camera*, and open the **Camera** app. Click **Allow** if asked. Check you can see yourself, with your face lit and your head and shoulders in the picture.
2. Move the lamp or yourself until your face is clear. Close the **Camera** app.
3. Make a short WhatsApp video call to a family member or friend who's expecting it.
4. During the call, press mute, ask "can you hear me?", and see them shake their head. Unmute. Turn your camera off and on.
5. End the call with the red button.

You've done it when you've muted, unmuted, turned your camera off and back on, and hung up on purpose.

## Quiz

```quiz
- q: The microphone button has a line through it. What does that mean?
  options:
    - You're muted; nobody can hear you
    - The microphone is broken
    - The call is being recorded
  answer: 0
  why: A line through the microphone means it's switched off (muted). Click or tap it to unmute.
- q: On a video call you appear as a dark shape. What's the most likely fix?
  options:
    - Lean in closer to the camera
    - Turn so the light is in front of you, not behind you
    - Leave the call and start again
  answer: 1
  why: A bright window or lamp behind you makes the camera darken everything. Face the light instead.
- q: Your browser asks to use your camera and microphone when you join a Zoom link the doctor sent. What do you do?
  options:
    - Click Block, to be safe
    - Close the browser
    - Click Allow
  answer: 2
  why: The call can't use your camera and microphone without permission. Allowing it for a meeting link you were expecting is fine.
```

## Scribble watch

The Scribble sends a video call link from "the bank" or "Microsoft support", and once you've joined, asks you to share your screen or install a little helper program. Real banks and tech companies don't do this. Only join calls from people you were expecting, and never share your screen or install anything because someone on a call asked you to. Just press **Leave**.
