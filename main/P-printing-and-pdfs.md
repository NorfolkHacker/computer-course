## The Operator says

I hate printers. They jam. They run out of ink you didn't know they had. They go "offline" while sitting a foot from the computer. If I could make every printer in the land vanish, I'd do it before lunch.

Until then: print once, and wait. Better still, learn PDFs: paper without the printer. Most forms and letters can be sent as a PDF and never touch the beast.

## The Mender's way

Three things: printing on paper, making and opening **PDFs**, and turning paper into a file with your phone (**scanning**).

### Adding a printer to Windows

Switch the printer on. A Wi-Fi printer must join your Wi-Fi first (see its own instructions). Then:

1. Click **Start**, then **Settings** (the gear icon).
2. Click **Bluetooth & devices** on the left.
3. Click **Printers & scanners**.
4. Click **Add device** at the top right. Wait while Windows looks.
5. When your printer's name appears, click **Add device** next to it.

If the printer doesn't appear, check it's on and on the same Wi-Fi, then click **Add device** again.

### Printing

1. Open the document, web page or photo.
2. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>P</kbd> (or **File**, then **Print**).
3. A print window opens. The large picture is the **print preview**: exactly how the pages will look on paper. Check it. Is everything on the page? How many pages?
4. Check the **Printer** box shows your printer's name.
5. Choose the number of **copies** (normally 1) and, if you only want some pages, set **Pages**.
6. Click **Print** **once**. Then wait. Printers are slow to wake up.

If nothing comes out after a minute, don't click Print again. Check the printer is on, has paper and shows no warning light.

### What a PDF is

A **PDF** is a kind of file that looks exactly the same on every computer and phone, like a printed page you can still search. Forms, tickets and bills usually come as PDFs, and they can't easily be changed by accident.

### Making a PDF

**From anything that prints:**

1. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>P</kbd>.
2. In the **Printer** box, choose **Microsoft Print to PDF** (in the Edge or Chrome browser it may be called **Save as PDF**).
3. Click **Print** (or **Save**).
4. A save window opens. Type a sensible name, check it's going into **Documents**, and click **Save**.

**From Word:** **File**, then **Save As**, choose a folder, and change **Save as type** to **PDF**. **From Google Docs:** **File**, then **Download**, then **PDF Document (.pdf)**. It goes into **Downloads**.

### Opening a PDF

Double-click it. On Windows 11, PDFs open in **Microsoft Edge**, where you can read, zoom, print and fill in many forms. You don't need to buy or download anything extra.

On Android, tap the PDF; it opens in **Drive** or your phone's own PDF viewer.

### Scanning paper with your phone

You don't need a scanner. Your phone's camera can turn a paper page into a neat PDF.

**With Google Drive** (on almost every Android phone):

1. Open the **Drive** app.
2. Tap the **Camera** button (bottom right, just above **+ New**). On older versions, tap **+** then **Scan**.
3. Allow the camera if asked.
4. Lay the paper flat on a dark table in good light. Hold the phone straight above it and press the shutter button.
5. To add another page, tap **Add** (the **+** icon) and take another photo.
6. Tap **Done**, give it a name, check **PDF** is chosen, and tap **Save**. It's now in your Google Drive, and you can open it on your PC at **drive.google.com**.

**With the camera:** on many phones, pointing the **Camera** app at a document brings up a **Scan** button or a document outline. Tap it. Names vary by maker; if you don't see it, use Drive.

### On Windows (printing from the PC)

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>P</kbd> and follow the printing steps above.

### On Android (printing from your phone)

Your phone can print straight to a Wi-Fi printer on the same network.

1. Open the photo, PDF or document.
2. Tap the **⋮** (three dots) menu, then **Print**. In some apps it's under **Share**, then **Print**.
3. At the top, tap **Select a printer** and choose yours. If it isn't listed, tap **All printers** and look for it.
4. Check the preview, then tap the round printer button.

If no printers appear, open **Settings** and search for `Printing`, and make sure **Default Print Service** is turned on. Some printers also want their own app from the Play Store.

## Do it now

1. Open any web page you like.
2. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>P</kbd> and look at the print preview. Count the pages.
3. Choose **Microsoft Print to PDF** (or **Save as PDF**) as the printer.
4. Save it as `My first PDF` in **Documents**.
5. Open **Documents** (<kbd>Windows</kbd> + <kbd>E</kbd>) and double-click `My first PDF`.

You've done it when the web page opens as a PDF in Edge, and no paper was harmed.

## Scribble watch

The Scribble disguises itself as "Free PDF Reader! Download now!" adverts and emails saying "Your PDF invoice is attached". Windows already reads PDFs in Edge, and Android reads them in Drive, so you never need to download a PDF program from an advert. And a PDF you weren't expecting, from someone you don't know, gets deleted, not opened.
